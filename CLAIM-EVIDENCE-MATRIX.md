# Claim–evidence matrix

| Claim class | Exact scope | Written evidence | Executable evidence | Falsification control | Non-claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independent release windows | Declared closed intervals and declared reserved-service semantics at one bottleneck | Main theorem and full proof | Existing bounded enumerator and witness checker | Endpoint-only baseline and semantic-calendar counterexample | Not arbitrary correlated releases or work-conserving borrowing |
| Shared versus private buffer | Same uncertainty set and service model | Capacity corollaries and attaining witnesses | Pooled/private comparison checks | Sum-of-private-peaks counterexample | Not a universal rule for every queueing discipline |
| Private-cap admission | Per-tenant cap and feasible rate domain in the paper | Exact inversion/cap theorem | Existing cap checker | Boundary and infeasible-cap cases | Not a transport-control law |
| Finite affine allocation | Finite envelope, rate bounds, fixed total reservation | LP/duality/KKT theorem | Production path plus independent exact rational oracle | Corrupted rate, objective, and multiplier certificates | Not a claim that vertex enumeration is scalable |
| Correlated finite-domain phases | Explicit factors, finite domains, chosen elimination order | Candidate-time and max-sum reduction lemmas | Production path plus independent factor-table VE and brute force | Invalid witness, invalid table, invalid order | Not arbitrary continuous correlation |
| Width-parameterized tractability | Domain size and induced width both appear in bound | VE theorem | Table-size trace; long bounded-width path | Clique/cycle growth cases | Not polynomial time at unbounded width |
| Hardness boundary | Decision language and restricted construction stated in paper | Membership and reduction proof | Exhaustive small-graph checker | Global-maximum comparison, not one sampled epoch | Not empirical evidence of typical-case hardness |
| Reproducibility | Delivered source and local inputs | READMEs and audit methodology | Clean extraction, tests, two-part reproduction, rebuild | Hash-seed changes and certificate mutation | Not third-party replication |
| Bibliographic identity | Entries actually cited in the manuscript | Citation-use ledger | DOI/identifier metadata audit | Mismatches and network failures remain visible | Not an endorsement of every cited conclusion |
